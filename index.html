Roomy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

The short version

Roomy helps you review and clean up the photos and videos on your own device. Your photos are processed on your device. We do not upload, store, sell or share them, and we do not collect personal information.

This policy explains how the Roomy app (“Roomy”, “the app”), provided by Orbilo.uk (“we”, “us”), handles information.

1. Access to your photos and videos

To work, Roomy asks for permission to access your photo library. We use this access only to:

  • show your photos and videos inside the app so you can review them;
  • find duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, screen recordings and large files on your device;
  • let you keep, share or delete items when you choose to.

All of this happens on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party by Roomy.

2. Deleting photos

Nothing is deleted without your action. When you choose to delete items, your device asks you to confirm. On iPhone, deleted items normally move to the “Recently Deleted” album, where they stay for a period set by Apple before being permanently removed. You can restore them from there.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts or the content of your photos. Roomy does not require you to create an account.

Roomy does not contain advertising, does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not collect crash reports. The app is currently free to use and does not offer purchases or subscriptions.

4. Sharing

When you use the Share button, the item you selected is passed to the app or service you choose through your device’s share sheet. That sharing is your decision, and the receiving app’s privacy policy applies to it.

5. Children’s privacy

Roomy does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Security

Because your photos stay on your device and we do not collect your data, the main protection is your own device’s security. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with a passcode.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions about your privacy rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at zaid@orbilo.uk.
